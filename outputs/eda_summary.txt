TELECOM TRAFFIC EXPLORATORY DATA ANALYSIS SUMMARY
============================================================

1. DIURNAL TRAFFIC PATTERNS:
   - Peak internet activity occurs at approximately 16:00.
   - Minimum traffic (trough) occurs during nighttime at 04:00.
   - Traffic exhibits a clear human behavioral cycle matching typical work and leisure hours.

2. MODALITY CORRELATIONS:
   - Pearson correlation with Call-in: 0.795
   - Pearson correlation with Call-out: 0.799
   - Pearson correlation with SMS-in: 0.788
   - Pearson correlation with SMS-out: 0.750

3. KEY OBSERVATIONS:
   - Voice and SMS volume moderately to strongly co-occur with data consumption.
   - Time-of-day (hour) is a crucial non-linear feature for activity estimation.
